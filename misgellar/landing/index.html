Misgellar

Misgellar ("мизгелләр" in Tatar - "moments") is a simple, focused film photography companion for keeping track of every frame you shoot.

Download on the App Store Download Misgellar film photography notes app from the App Store

Film notes, ready when the scans come back

Create a roll for each camera, record exposure settings and location while you shoot, attach a reference image when useful, and keep your notes ready for the moment the film comes back from the lab.

The capture screen works like a small notebook and a simple light meter in one place: choose aperture priority, shutter priority, or manual mode, adjust focal length, aperture, shutter speed, and exposure compensation, then save the shot with timestamp and optional GPS location.

When your scans are ready, Misgellar can help match them back to your recorded shots and write selected metadata into the image files, including camera, film stock, ISO, exposure settings, capture date, and location.

  • Track multiple film rolls across different cameras
  • Record shutter speed, aperture, focal length, exposure compensation, date, and location
  • Attach reference photos, or record shots without using the camera
  • Support for colour, black and white, and redscale rolls
  • View shot locations on a map
  • Import and share .msglr film roll bundles
  • Store rolls locally or in iCloud Drive
  • Match recorded shots with film scans from Photos or Files
  • Automatic scan matching using Computer Vision with manual adjustments when needed
  • Split half-frame scans before matching
  • Save or export scans with selected EXIF and GPS metadata

Perfect for film photographers who want a clean shooting log, a simple exposure helper, and an easier way to bring analogue notes back into their scanned photos.
